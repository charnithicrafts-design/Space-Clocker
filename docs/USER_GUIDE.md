# 🚀 Space-Clocker User Guide & Operations Manual

Welcome to **Space-Clocker**, a high-fidelity, space-themed productivity and skill-tracking platform designed to help technical professionals, students, and ambitious creators construct clear trajectories toward their macro-goals.

---

## 🌟 Core Philosophy: Trajectory Over Friction

Productivity tools often treat work like an inescapable assembly line, punishing you for missed days and creating anxiety around incomplete tasks. 

Space-Clocker is built on a different premise: **human performance is a trajectory through deep space**.
- **Orbit** is where you navigate your daily tasks and schedule.
- **Nebula** is where you architect 1 to 3-year stellar horizons and milestones.
- **Stellar Matrix** maps your technical competencies and personal growth as a constellation.
- **Horizon** acts as your mission control telemetry dashboard.
- **Gravity Wells & Orbital Decay** replace harsh failure metrics with realistic, self-compassionate recalibration tools.

---

## 🛸 The Four Operational Modes

### 1. ⚡ Orbit (Daily Scheduler & Mission Control)
Orbit is your primary cockpit for daily task management, focus sessions, and real-time scheduling.

* **Daily Mission Log:** View, complete, and manage tasks planned for today.
* **Stasis Backlog:** A dedicated holding area for pending or deferred items.
* **`[⚡ Recalibrate]` Mechanism:** If a task drifts past its planned date without being completed, it enters *Orbital Decay*. Instead of marking you as failed, Space-Clocker displays an amber glowing `Recalibrate` button. Clicking it instantly realigns the task to today's active schedule with smooth audio feedback.
* **Void Protocol:** An integrated anti-habit tracker. Declare your major distractions (e.g., *doom-scrolling*, *over-refining finished work*), set max engagement limits, and monitor your resistance.

### 2. 🌌 Nebula (Stellar Architect Mode)
Nebula is where macro-ambitions are decomposed into actionable steps.

* **Macro-Ambitions:** Define long-term horizons (e.g., *AWS Specialist Certification*, *Mastering Structural Engineering*).
* **Stellar Milestones:** Break macro-ambitions into sub-goals and specific target dates.
* **Linked Skills:** Connect specific milestones directly to your radar chart skills so every task completed builds tangible proficiency.

### 3. 💎 Stellar Matrix (Skills Matrix)
The Stellar Matrix transforms your competencies into an interactive radar chart visualization.

* **Bright Stars:** Skills with high proficiency (>80%) shine as bright navigational nodes.
* **Personal & Ambition Skills:** Every professional profile tracks both hard technical skills (e.g., *Cloud Architecture*, *Surgical Precision*) and personal resilience skills (e.g., *Deep Work Focus*, *Stress Regulation*).
* **Gap Analysis:** Visually compare your current proficiency against target goals to identify where to focus your focus hours.

### 4. 🔭 Horizon (Telemetry & Analytics Dashboard)
Horizon provides high-level telemetry on your productivity velocity over 7, 30, and 365 days.

* **Stellar Activity Heatmap:** An area chart showing task completion momentum over time.
* **Gravity Wells Analysis:** Visualizes distraction frequency without judgment.
* **Temporal Vulnerability Radar:** Identifies specific hours of the day when you are most susceptible to focus decay.
* **Reactor Core Gauge:** Shows raw XP accumulated and percentage progress toward your next level.
* **Eleanor's Log:** A context-aware empathetic intelligence panel that reads your telemetry state and provides personalized, compassionate advice.

## 🛠️ Daily Workflow Recommendations

1. **Morning Alignment (08:00):** Open **Orbit**. Review today's mission log and check the **Stasis Backlog**. Click `[⚡ Recalibrate]` on any decayed tasks you wish to pull into today's flight plan.
2. **Deep Work Phase:** Complete tasks in Orbit. Track your focus hours and listen for satisfying audio feedback upon completion.
3. **Weekly Check-in:** Navigate to **Nebula** and **Stellar Matrix**. Review milestone progression and see your radar chart expand as your skills gain XP.
4. **Monthly Review:** Visit **Horizon** to view your overall momentum over 30 or 365 days and read **Eleanor's Log** for burnout prevention insights.
